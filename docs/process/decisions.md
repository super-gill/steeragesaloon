# Decisions

Lifecycle: **living**

The index of every settled decision, newest first: the date, the decision in a few words, and where it lives. Each is recorded in full in the page it concerns, under "Decision record". A superseded decision is struck through and points at the one that replaced it.

**Adding one:** write it into its page (dated, who decided), then add its line here, at the top, in the same commit.

Decisions before 17 November 2026 were gathered from the old roadmap, the known-issues log and the 1900 overhaul notes (`docs/archive/`) when the project adopted `vibe-process`; their dates are the release that recorded them.
