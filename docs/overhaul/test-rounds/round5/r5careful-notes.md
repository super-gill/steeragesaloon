## Strategy and state left (1 January 1940)
Strategy: one old emigrant ship in 1900; moved to Southampton then Liverpool ↔ Halifax; bought cheap old ships (Caledonian 1903, Silver Wave 1905, Hesperia 1908, Wendland 1913) and built 11,000grt 14kn emigrant ships (Lochiel 1907, Lochaber 1910, Lochnagar 1912, Lochinver 1913); joined the conference 1908; piers at Halifax, Liverpool (and New York 1936); debt-free from 1912. War: zigzag, guns, lay-ups, let the Admiralty take six ships. 1919-20: sold old ships to the syndicates. 1921-25: two new emigrant ships, bought Veere and Strathnevis, Tourist Third refits, £1m Consols. 1927 and 1935: tourist ships Iona, Islay; Mull 1938. Bought Glenorchy (1934), Chesapeake (1935), Auchrannoch (1938) cheap. Departments: Marine, Crewing (from 1909) and Fares (from 1922), all acting.
State: cash £7,283,861, debt £0, Consols £4,127,841, net worth £14,799,077, reputation 58. 11 ships: on lha Lochnagar (1912), Lochinver (1913), Glencoe (1923), Glenfinnan (1924), Chesapeake (1932), Mull (1938); on liv Veere (1921), Iona (1927), Islay (1935), Auchrannoch (1934); Glenorchy (1921) cruising the West Indies (cwx) for the winter, back to lha in April. Mail on lha and liv. Out of the conference: the Combine expelled the Line in Aug 1938 and its rate war runs to Sept 1940 (S.combine.until 488); `join` refused until then.
